## Start here

Before working on this project, read **`docs/STATUS.md`** — it has the current
status, the ordered TODO/backlog of remaining work, the stack, run/test/deploy
commands, and hard-won gotchas. Then consult `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md` (the product +
engineering spec, v2.1) and `docs/SECURITY_SPEC.md` as needed. Per-feature
designs and implementation plans live under `docs/superpowers/`.
